The Hidden Statistics of Hollywood

An Interactive Exploration of Films Throughout History

By: Ritwik Behera, Neil Deng, Richard Gancayco, Merliss George, and Bill Zhou

Background

Since the invention of film cinematography, movies have evolved in response to various societal shifts, advancements in technology, and changes in the popular cultural landscape. These shifts in film trends may seem random when looking at the film industry as a whole, but if you observe the minute details, you can find patterns that reveal a greater story. You can make interpolations as to what cultural phenomena were taking place at the time to lead to an increase or decrease in popularity for certain story genres. This exploratory interactive visualization will allow you to do just that.

The interactive visualization below will allow you to examine and analyze trends in film media through various different lenses, such as average rating, box office sales, along with other relevant factors. Through the Dot Plot, you can observe different trends in the popularity of specific genres of films. You can also analyze trends in various movie franchises or the works of different directors throughout their careers using a variety of metrics.

Using the Stream Graph, you can observe the volume of the genres of movies that were produced throughout the years or which movie genres seemed to dominate during different eras in history. You can also visually analyze when specific genres experienced declines in popularity and draw conclusions as to what was occurring during that time period that would cause such a shift.

Additionally, as you are exploring different aspects of film media through time, you can also analyze various summary statistics relevant to the data the appears on the graphs such as mean, standard deviation, etc.

Summary

As seen above, we have the ability to visualize a large amount of global film data that can then be used to make inferences about various trends and shifts in the cultural landscape during different time periods. We can see how certain genres rose in popularity or fell into obscurity by looking at their box office performances or their critical review ratings.
